# Turtle Graphics Demo

## To run interactively:
./gradlew run

## Example session:
Turtle Graphics Controller
Type 'quit' to exit

> move 100
Turtle at (100.0, 0.0), heading 0.0°, pen down

> turn 90
Turtle at (100.0, 0.0), heading 90.0°, pen down

> move 100
Turtle at (100.0, 100.0), heading 90.0°, pen down

> turn 90
Turtle at (100.0, 100.0), heading 180.0°, pen down

> move 100
Turtle at (0.0, 100.0), heading 180.0°, pen down

> turn 90
Turtle at (0.0, 100.0), heading 270.0°, pen down

> move 100
Turtle at (0.0, 0.0), heading 270.0°, pen down

> trace
Drawing path:
  (0.0, 0.0) -> (100.0, 0.0)
  (100.0, 0.0) -> (100.0, 100.0)
  (100.0, 100.0) -> (0.0, 100.0)
  (0.0, 100.0) -> (0.0, 0.0)

> quit
Goodbye!

## The turtle drew a square!
